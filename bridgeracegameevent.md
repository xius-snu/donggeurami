@Game time, Event

Before   Join for 50 coins. 10 s after the last player joins, bots fill the empty seats. Then a 5 s countdown

0:00   Go! Everyone starts with 10m of bridge

0:12   First grant of bridge (and maybe dynamite). Then another grant every 12 s

6:00   Time's up: the team with more players on the golden island wins. If equal, the team whose bridge got closer. If still equal, a draw

Any time   Win: ALL of a team stands on the golden island together for 3 s. (Most races end in about 70 s)



###### Every grant (every 12 s):

Bridge: 8-16m per team (random), split between its players (1v1 = 8-16m each, 2v2 = 4-8m each)

Dynamite: 30% chance per player (about 1 every 40 s) | max held = 2

The plate under the timer shows your next grant: "Next Item: 14m", plus a dynamite icon if one is coming



###### Items (no shop):

Bridge: turn the plank button on and walk. Costs its length in metres

Dynamite: hold & drag & let go to throw. Breaks the planks in its blast at once, then the whole connected bridge falls plank by plank (same as Bridge Wars). Knocks back the other team only

Punch: knockback only. Nobody has health



###### Golden island:

Get on it by bridge, or jump the last gap (a running jump clears about 6.5m). A bridge all the way is not needed

Being knocked off, or jumping, breaks the hold and the 3 s starts again



###### Falling into the void:

The only way to die. Back on the center of your own island after 2 s. You keep your bridge and dynamite



###### Teams (Red vs Blue):

1 real player: 1v1 against a bot

2 real players: 1/3 same team vs 2 bots | 1/3 opposite teams, each with a bot | 1/3 1v1 with no bots

3 real players: 2 players vs 1 player + 1 bot

4 real players: 2v2, no bots



###### Coins:

Entry: 50

Win: 110 each (+60) | Lose: 0 (-50) | Draw: 50 back
