@Game time / Wave, Event

Before   Join for 50 coins. 1-4 players, no bots. Starts 15 s after the last player joins (at once with 4). Then a 5 s countdown

0:00   Go! Generators make silver only. Anchor crystal = 1500 HP

0:20   Wave 1: walkers

Wave 3   Runners appear

Wave 4   Brutes appear

Wave 5   A giant. Then giants every 5th wave (1 in waves 5 and 10, 2 in waves 15 and 20, 3 in waves 25 and 30 ...)

Wave 8   Spitters appear

Wave 12   Leapers appear

Between waves   25 s break to shop. If everyone alive presses Ready, the next wave comes in 3 s

Anchor broken   Nobody respawns anymore

Game over   Anchor broken and nobody alive. The waves never end



###### Every wave is harder:

Zombies get +22% health, +9% damage, +1% speed (speed max +30%)

More zombies: wave 1 = 9 (1 player) / 16 (4 players) | wave 5 = 27 / 48 | wave 10 = 61 / 107

Max 40 zombies alive at once, the rest wait their turn



###### Zombies (numbers are wave 1's):

Walker (wave 1): 20 HP, 6 dmg, slow

Runner (wave 3): 12 HP, 5 dmg, fast

Brute (wave 4): 48 HP, 14 dmg, slow. Knocks you back, hits the anchor harder, always drops 2-3 loot

Giant (every 5th wave): 450 HP, 28 dmg, slowest. Always goes for the anchor, cannot be knocked back, drops 12 loot

Spitter (wave 8): 18 HP, 10 dmg. Stays 7-9m away and spits a slow green ball you can walk out of

Leaper (wave 12): 25 HP, 12 dmg. Leaps at you from a few metres away

They come out of the sea and go for the anchor or the nearest player. A zombie you hit turns on you



###### Players:

100 HP. Heals 4 HP per second after 5 s without being hit

Damage: fist 6 | Basic Sword 14 | Silver Sword 22 | Aura Sword 34

Death: back next to the anchor after 6 s (only while the anchor stands)

On death you drop ALL your silver, gold, sapphire, amethyst and your sword on the ground. You or a teammate can pick it up (a sword only if it is better than the one held). It disappears after 5 minutes

You keep your shield, the items on your buttons and all team upgrades

Map: sand in the sea. You can wade in the sea but an invisible ring stops everyone about 4m out



###### Generators (2 pads next to the anchor):

Silver: 1 per ~2 sec | max held on a pad = 24

Gold: 1 per ~8 sec | max held = 6 (needs Gold Forge)

Sapphire: 1 per ~30 sec | max held = 3 (needs Sapphire Forge)

Amethyst: 1 per ~60 sec | max held = 2 (needs Amethyst Forge)

More players = faster: 1 player 100%, 2 players 140%, 3 players 180%, 4 players 220%



###### Zombie loot:

1 in 3 zombies drops 1 thing (brute 2-3, giant 12)

Mostly silver. More gold every wave. Sapphire from wave 3. Amethyst from wave 5



###### Treasure chest:

One at a time, no notification, on the sand or in the sea. First one 45-120 s after Go!, the next one 75-160 s after the last one is gone. Disappears after 5 minutes

Break it open: Aura Sword 1 hit | Basic / Silver Sword 2 hits | fist 3 hits

Its loot bursts out onto the ground: silver 10-25 (32%) | gold 2-6 (34%) | sapphire 1-3 (18%) | amethyst 1-2 (9%) | the next sword up (7%)

Amounts grow 8% every wave



###### Item Shop (Red) (yours, kept on death except the sword):

Basic Sword: 12 silver (14 dmg)

Silver Sword: 8 gold (22 dmg)

Aura Sword: 6 sapphire (34 dmg)

Basic Shield: 20 silver (10% dmg reduction)

Advanced Shield: 10 gold (20% dmg reduction)

Aura Shield: 5 sapphire (30% dmg reduction)

(swords and shields: you can only buy a better one than you have)

Healing Potion: 4 gold (heals 50% of your health)

Speed Potion: 1 sapphire (+40% speed for 30 sec)

Leap Potion: 1 sapphire (5x jump height for 45 sec)

Invisibility Potion: 2 sapphire (zombies lose you for 20 sec)

Fireball: 30 silver (40 blast dmg)

Dynamite: 1 amethyst (120 blast dmg + big knockback)

(no Bridge Builder in this game)



###### Upgrade Shop (Blue) (for the whole team):

Generator: Gold Forge 24 silver -> Sapphire Forge 12 gold -> Amethyst Forge 8 sapphire -> Overclock (endless): 4, 6, 9, 14, 20, 30 ... amethyst (each level = generators 25% faster)

Sharpness (endless): 6, 9, 13, 18, 27, 38 ... gold (+12% damage per level, on swords, fists, fireballs and dynamite)

Protection (max 5): 20, 32, 51, 82, 131 silver (8% dmg reduction per level)

Fortify Anchor (endless): 24, 38, 61, 98, 157 ... silver (+250 anchor max HP and heals the anchor to full)

Anchor Beam (endless): 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15 ... sapphire (every 2 sec the anchor zaps the nearest zombie within 12m for 12 dmg per level)

Lucky Loot (max 5): 5, 8, 13, 20, 33 gold (+10% drop chance per level, and better loot)



###### Score:

Kill (last hit): walker 10 | runner 15 | spitter 25 | leaper 30 | brute 40 | giant 250 (+10% per wave number)

+1 for every 10 damage you deal

Team score = everyone's score + 100 per wave cleared



###### Coins:

Entry: 50

Everyone gets 10 coins per wave cleared, max 400 (5 waves = your 50 back)
