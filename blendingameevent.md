@Round time, Event

Before   Online only. Join for 50 coins. 2-4 real players, no bots. Starts 15 s after the 2nd player joins (at once with 4). Then a 5 s countdown

0:00   Choose (max 30 s): everyone picks boy/girl + skin + name. Ends early when everyone presses Ready

0:30   Hide (15 s): the hider is put among the town people. Seekers have a black screen and cannot move

0:45   Seek (2 min 30 s): seekers hunt for the hider. Ends at once if the hider is found

3:15   Round over (6 s): a column of light shows where the hider was and who they were disguised as

3:21   Next round with the next hider. After the last round: results

One round per player, so everyone hides once. The order is random



###### Hider:

Disguised as one of the town people (a random look and name is offered, you can change it)

Walks at 7 m/s like the crowd. No sword

+1 point for staying hidden until the timer runs out



###### Seeker:

Holds the Aura Sword and has the speed potion effect for the whole seek (14 m/s = 2x the hider, with blue sparks)

Has a soft glow on the ground, so the hider can see seekers coming

Hit the hider: found. +1 point to the finder

Hit a town person: wrong guess. Blinded and frozen for 4 sec, then 7, then 10 (+3 sec every time, resets each round)



###### Map:

Round Town, inside a ring 70m around the fountain (nobody can leave it)

28 town people (AI) walk around with new names and looks every round

Chat is off from Hide until the end of Seek



###### Leaving:

Hider leaves (or their app is gone for 20 sec): the round is given up, no point

Anyone who leaves finishes last

Fewer than 2 players left: the game ends



###### Winning:

Most points wins. Ties share the place

2 players: both found, or both stayed hidden = draw



###### Coins:

Entry: 50

2 players: 1st 100 | 2nd 0 | draw = 50 each

3 players: 1st 110 | 2nd 40 | 3rd 0

4 players: 1st 120 | 2nd 70 | 3rd 10 | 4th 0



No shop, no items.
